I am a seasoned data and information systems specialist with over 15 years of experience in ICT, monitoring & evaluation (M&E), and data management across humanitarian, development, and academic sectors. My academic background includes a BSc. in Mathematics and Computer Science (First Class Honours) and an MSc. in ICT Policy & Regulation, complemented by advanced studies in Environmental Science.

Throughout my career, I have designed and implemented innovative data solutions that strengthen program accountability, resilience measurement, and evidence-based decision-making. I have worked with leading organizations including Mercy Corps, World Vision, UNHCR, Concern Worldwide, and the World Bank, where I have led the development of large-scale M&E systems, secure health and protection databases, and geospatial analytics platforms for hard-to-reach regions. Notably, I created **STANDARDIZE**, a data sandbox portal supporting resilience research and geospatial mapping in Somalia, and co-led the global rollout of **TolaData**, an adaptive program management system used across multiple countries.

My expertise spans data visualization, dashboard development, secure data pipelines, GIS/remote sensing, and multi-agency collaboration. I have a proven track record of integrating complex datasets, building real-time monitoring portals, and delivering donor reporting aligned with key program indicators. I thrive on bridging technology and humanitarian action—leveraging ICT innovations to improve program outcomes, strengthen accountability, and drive social impact in fragile and conflict-affected contexts.
