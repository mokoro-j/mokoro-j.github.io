I am a data and information systems specialist with over 15 years of experience in ICT, monitoring & evaluation (M&E), and data management across humanitarian, development, and academic sectors. With a strong academic foundation in Mathematics, Computer Science, and ICT Policy, I have designed and implemented innovative data solutions that enhance program accountability, resilience measurement, and evidence-based decision-making.

My career spans leadership roles in international organizations such as Mercy Corps, World Vision, Danish Refugee Council/UNHCR, Concern Worldwide, and the World Bank, where I have built and managed large-scale M&E systems, integrated health and protection databases, and developed geospatial analytics platforms for hard-to-reach regions. Notably, I created **STANDARDIZE**, a data sandbox portal supporting resilience research and geospatial mapping in Somalia, and have led the rollout of adaptive program management tools like **TolaData** across multiple countries.

I bring expertise in data visualization, dashboard development, secure data pipelines, and GIS/remote sensing, with a proven track record of strengthening multi-agency collaboration and donor reporting. My work consistently bridges technology and social impact—leveraging ICT innovations to improve humanitarian programming, policy implementation, and resilience outcomes.
